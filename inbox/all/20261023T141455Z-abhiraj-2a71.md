1. Holm families
   - E1: Delta(32) - Delta(1). E2: field(32) - field(1).
   - Each family runs across models x datasets on two arms: uniform and decoder:topk_conf:k4.
   - With PR #9 that is 7 model-dataset arms, so 14 tests per family: LladaBase, DreamBase, LladaInstructGsm, LladaInstructMbpp, DreamInstructGsm, DreamInstructMbpp, Mdlm.
   - Exploratory, outside the families: decoder k = 2 and k = 8, and uniform_matched.
   - The contamination-clean and time-split runs are robustness checks, also outside the families.
   - gamma_2..gamma_4: Holm within each arm, as now.
2. Context calibrator: the interacted form (ell x field features), which is the code default. Plan v2 T6(2)'s additive form is the sensitivity check.
3. Splits: the FineWeb rule (c3.data.fineweb.document_split, seed 20260917) applied to every set.
   - GSM8K: artifacts/split_manifest_gsm8k.json, 660 fit / 659 report.
   - MBPP: artifacts/split_manifest_mbpp.json, 250 / 250.
   - Time-split: artifacts/split_manifest_timesplit.json, 250 / 250.
4. T8 temperature: log-odds per mask-rate bin, c = sigma(ell / T), is the primary row. Softmax (rebuilt from the top-20 probabilities plus an even tail) is a sensitivity row.
   - Why: in fit-half development runs (jobs 18932 and 18974, LLaDA-Base) softmax fitted T = 0.86-0.95 in every bin, sharpening a nearly calibrated model. Log-odds gave T = 0.94-1.05.

   - E3 for top-k decoders only;

   - MBPP test 482 excluded.
6. New deviation: the Eq. 2/3 bootstrap (beta_z, gamma_k) runs at 1,000 resamples, not plan v2 Sec. 5's 10,000.
   - It runs in the primary run and both knot runs. The contamination-clean and time-split runs report E1/E2 only (--skip-regression).
   - Everything else stays at 10,000, including the E1 and E2 contrasts that make up the Holm families (they use the cheap reweighting bootstrap).
   - Why: the refit costs ~3.7 s per resample on 150 documents with 8 CPUs. At 10,000 that is ~70 h per run on ~1,000 documents, and analysis_commands.sh has ~20 such runs.
