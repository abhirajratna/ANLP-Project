WHAT C1 IS
Diffusion LMs fill blanks, and the decoder commits the guesses it is most confident about. If you always pick the most confident guesses, some won by luck, so their confidence overstates their accuracy: a winner's curse.
- E1 and E2 measure whether that happens.
- E3 asks whether the states a real decoder visits are less calibrated than random ones.
- T8 rules out the boring explanations: position, token type, temperature, and 4-bit vs fp16.
