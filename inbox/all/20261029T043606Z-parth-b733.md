VERDICT: no output-corrupting bug. The behaviour is real for KLASS at these knobs, but the knobs are far outside the paper's operating point.
- Faithful to the paper and reference code (shkim0116/KLASS llada_klass.py): stability rule KL<eps for 2 steps and conf>tau (c3/decode/policies.py:128-139), commit all stable tokens, fallback of 1 token. At tau=.6, eps=.03 we get 2.58 tok/step; the paper's tau=.6, eps=.015 gives 256/98.6=2.60. Slot 1 scores .791.
- Divergence A: c3/decode/loop.py:127-143 computes KL only over the active block. Every 32-token block starts with no history and forces 2 one-token steps; the reference keeps history over all positions and forces only 1. At ~4 tok/step that is 16 of ~64 steps.
- Divergence B: the KL direction is reversed: ours KL(prev||now), reference KL(now||prev). Minor.
- Divergence C: our blocks are 32; the paper uses 64. This applies to every decoder.
- Why slot 2 is low: to reach ~4 tok/step, the fit-half calibration had to drop tau to .29 (eps .17, ~11x the paper's). There KLASS commits low-confidence neighbours together. 78% of slot-2 completions have doubled or runaway words ('toys toys toys', '\boxed444'), against 0% for C3a and threshold at ~4 tok/step. KLASS is wrong where C3a is right on 212/659 problems, the reverse on 25. NFE per problem is not degenerate. Accuracy tracks tau, not speed. Fit-half accuracy at these knobs can't be checked, because the calibration kept only speeds.
