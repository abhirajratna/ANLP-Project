2. Units. Use fractions with 3 decimals, as C1 does. C1 writes every non-count macro on its natural scale with 3 decimals: analyze_traces.py write_macros uses "{:.3f}" (e.g. \COneLladaInstructGsmECEDecTopkConfKFour = 0.394), c1_paper_outputs.py _est_macros uses nd=3, and counts are integers. Nothing in C1's output is a percent. score_t12.py also stores accuracy and its ci95 as fractions. So all of these are on the 0-1 scale with 3 decimals, and Lo/Hi likewise:
   - DevECERaw/DevECECal, MonotoneShare and OutsideShare;
   - Acc, MatchedDiff and Sanity{TopK,Threshold}Acc.
   N and FitDocs are integers. In the prose:
   - the published numbers become 0.793 / 0.792;
   - "2 points" becomes 0.02;
   - OutsideShare reads as "a fraction \Cres{...} of C3a's GSM8K steps".

3. CIs: yes, two kinds.
   - Per-configuration accuracy, including the sanity rows, uses the 95% Wilson interval. That is score_t12.py, wilson() with z = 1.959964, the ci95 field.
   - The matched difference C3a - decoder is paired on the shared doc_ids, with a 95% percentile interval from 2,000 bootstrap resamples of problems (deviation 17, "Matching").
   Suggested caption: "Accuracy with its 95% Wilson interval; paired difference C3a - decoder on the same problems, with a 95% percentile bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples of problems); positive favours C3a." The figure draws the Wilson intervals; say so in fig:c3-speed's caption. Deviation 17 has no Holm family and no declaration rule: T12 is exploratory, so please word the result sentences as estimates, not "significant".

Corrections to the T12 paragraph, from deviation 17:
   - Speed is not "mean tokens per step". It is aggregate tokens per step, 256 N / sum(NFE) over a configuration's N problems (total generated positions over total forward passes), not the mean of per-problem ratios. Please change "speed is measured as mean tokens per step", "closest in mean tokens per step" and the caption.
   - Knobs: each decoder's knob is calibrated per task, on fit-half problems only, to 2, 4 and 8 tokens per step. top-k, margin and PC-Sampler are exact at k = 2, 4, 8. Threshold, entropy-bounded, KLASS and C3a are interpolated in log(speed), and every knob is chosen by speed alone, never by fit-half accuracy.
   - KLASS: if it cannot reach 8 tokens per step within +-10%, its fastest setting fills the speed-3 slot and that comparison reads ---.
   - "This rule was fixed before any report-half number was read": add "(deviation 17)".
