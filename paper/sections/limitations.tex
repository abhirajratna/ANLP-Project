\section*{Limitations}

\paragraph{Scope.}
We audit two 8B-scale families and one 110M MDLM on FineWeb-Edu, GSM8K and MBPP, with model
type and text type confounded; the 7--8B models' C1 traces are in fp16, as the GPUs we traced on lack bfloat16. C2's
exact terms cover one synthetic distribution and one 110M fine-tune.

\paragraph{Design and estimators.}
C1 is teacher-forced: its states are those of an always-right decoder, not those after a free
decoder's own mistakes, and a valid rewording counts as an error. The field term is only as
context-aware as the state features of $\hat\pi(\ell,s)$: up to four, three in uniform states,
where the step is constant.

\paragraph{Contamination.}
The screen flags only verbatim recall, and the time-split set mixes exposure with domain. Only
the 110M model isolates the domain part. Our claims are about change with field size (E1, E2),
not levels; where we read levels we rely on the 110M arm, and 110M levels need not transfer to 8B
models.
