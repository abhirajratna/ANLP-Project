\section*{Limitations}

\paragraph{Scope.}
We audit two 8B-scale families and one 110M MDLM on FineWeb-Edu, GSM8K and MBPP, with model
type and text type confounded; the 8B models run in fp16, as our GPUs lack bfloat16. C2's
exact terms cover one synthetic distribution and one 110M fine-tune.

\paragraph{Design and estimators.}
C1 is teacher-forced: its states are those of an always-right decoder, not those after a free
decoder's own mistakes, and a valid rewording counts as an error. The field term is only as
context-aware as the four state features of $\hat\pi(\ell,s)$.

\paragraph{Contamination.}
The screen flags only verbatim recall, and the time-split set mixes exposure with domain. Only
the 110M model isolates the domain part, so levels rest on it and the 8B arms carry claims
about trends in field size.

\paragraph{Exploratory analyses and C3.}
Analyses outside the Holm families (decoder states at $k\in\{2,8\}$, matched uniform states,
$\beta_z$, $\gamma_k$, E3, T8, the clean and time-split runs) carry no claims. C3a is fitted on
teacher-forced states but applied to free-running generation, and compared on one model, with
greedy decoding, one seed and two tasks.
