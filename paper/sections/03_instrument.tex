\section{Measurement instrument}
\label{sec:instrument}

\subsection{Models and data}
\label{sec:models}

\paragraph{Models.}
We audit LLaDA-8B \citep{nie2025llada} and Dream-7B \citep{ye2025dream}, Base and Instruct, in
fp16 on two RTX 2080 Ti cards (no bfloat16). As neither discloses its pretraining data, we also
trained a 110M MDLM \citep{sahoo2024mdlm} on data we control: a bidirectional transformer
(\VivaMdlmLayers{} layers, width \VivaMdlmWidth{}, \VivaMdlmHeads{} heads), GPT-2 BPE plus a
mask token, sequence length \VivaMdlmSeqLen{} without packing, \VivaMdlmTokensB{}B tokens
(\VivaMdlmSteps{} steps) of FineWeb-Edu sample-10BT \citep{penedo2024fineweb}. Its training
stream excludes the calibration and reserve documents (by ID and by hashes of their text and
first 256 tokens) and matches no time-split document.

\subsection{Traces}
\label{sec:traces}

Each measurement is one forward pass on a true document with some positions masked, recording
at each masked position $v_i$, $c_i$, $\ell_i$, $a_i$ (\S\ref{sec:setup}), the true token's
probability, entropy, margin, the top-\VivaTopK{} and whether it was committed. Each document
gets three arms of states: \emph{uniform}, each position masked independently at rate
$t\in\{\VivaMaskRateMin,0.2,\dots,\VivaMaskRateMax\}$, \VivaMasksPerRate{} masks per rate, as
in training; \emph{decoder}, every state of a teacher-forced confidence top-$k$ trajectory,
$k\in\{\VivaDecoderK\}$, which starts fully masked, commits the $k$ most confident positions,
fills them with the \emph{true} tokens and repeats (the states a decoder visits if it is
always right); and \emph{matched uniform}, a uniform mask of each decoder state's size, so
only the pattern differs. That is \VivaPassesPerDoc{} passes per document. From the stored
trace, in every state with at least \VivaPoolMax{} masked positions we draw \VivaNPools{}
random pools of each size $m\in\{\VivaPoolSizes\}$, rank them by $\ell$ with random tie-breaks
(fp16 confidences saturate) and keep the top four ranks; $\Delta(1)$ is exact over all masked
positions of those states.

\subsection{Estimands}
\label{sec:estimands}

\paragraph{Calibrators.}
Both are fitted on the fit half. $\hat\pi(\ell)$ is isotonic in $\ell$. $\hat\pi(\ell,s)$ is
logistic in a restricted cubic spline of $\ell$ plus the state's field features (median $\ell$
of its masked positions, mask rate, $\log|M|$, decoding step) and their products with $\ell$, so
the slope varies too; the plan's additive form is a sensitivity check.
Averages are taken within a document, every state weighing the same, then across documents;
positions are weighted by $1/(|M_s|\times\text{states in their document})$ to match, which is the
$m=1$ population of Proposition~\ref{prop:split}.

\paragraph{E1, E2 and the native gap.}
E1 is $\Delta(m)$ \eqref{eq:gap}, with primary contrast $\Delta(\VivaPoolMax)-\Delta(1)$,
reported beside 15-bin equal-mass ECE and smooth ECE over all masked positions. E2 is the field
term $\E_m[\hat\pi(\ell)-\hat\pi(\ell,s)]$ of \eqref{eq:gapsplit}, with primary contrast
field$(\VivaPoolMax)-$field$(1)$. On the same winners and resamples, own miscalibration
$\E_m[c-\hat\pi(\ell)]$, the field term and $\E_m[\hat\pi(\ell,s)-a]$ sum to $\Delta$ exactly;
the last, zero under Proposition~\ref{prop:select}, is a specification check, beside the
intra-class correlation of residuals $a-\hat\pi(\ell,s)$ within states. The \emph{native} gap
is $c-a$ over tokens the decoder actually committed, grouped by $|M_t|$ at commit time.

\paragraph{Eq.~2/3 diagnostics.}
Eq.~2 is on pool winners, Eq.~3 on the top four ranks; $\eta_i=\alpha\,[+\alpha_s]+f(\ell_i)$:
\begin{align}
  \Pr(a_i{=}1) &= \sigma\bigl(\eta_i + \beta_z z_{r,m}\bigr), \label{eq:eqtwo}\\
  \Pr(a_i{=}1) &= \sigma\bigl(\eta_i + {\textstyle\sum_{k=2}^{4}}\gamma_k \1\{r{=}k\}\bigr),
  \label{eq:eqthree}
\end{align}
where $z_{r,m}$ is the expected $r$-th largest of $m$ standard normals and $f$ a restricted
cubic spline with \VivaKnots{} knots (5 and 13 as sensitivity), fitted on the fit half and
carried over as a fixed offset. Each is fitted without and with state fixed effects $\alpha_s$,
which absorb the field term because it lives between states: $\beta_z<0$ without them and
${\approx}0$ with them points to a field effect, $\beta_z\neq0$ with them to shared errors.
This reading is approximate (\S\ref{sec:validation}), making $\beta_z$ a diagnostic.

\paragraph{E3, trajectory shift.}
For each decoder arm we fit, on its positions and a reference arm's,
$\Pr(a{=}1)=\sigma(\alpha_b+f(\ell)+\textit{dec}\cdot(g_0+f'(\ell)))$, with $\alpha_b$ an intercept
per mask-rate bin (centred on $0.1,\dots,0.9$). The reference is the matched uniform arm
(secondary: the uniform grid, reweighted to the decoder's rate mix). The endpoint is
$\E_{\mathrm{dec}}[\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(\ell,b)-\pi_{\mathrm{dec}}(\ell,b)]$, positive if
decoder-state tokens are right less often at equal confidence and mask rate; $(g_0,f')$ are
tested jointly by a sup-$t$ test on the difference curve.

\paragraph{T8 controls.}
Each control gives the E1 and E2 contrasts without and with it, and their paired difference.
\emph{Position} (absolute position, distance to the nearest unmasked token,
PC-Sampler's position weight; \citealp{huang2025pcsampler}) and \emph{token type} (of the true
token: punctuation, stop-word or content) are covariates $x$: $\E_m[\pi(\ell)-\pi(\ell,x)]$
is removed from $\Delta(m)$ and $x$ enters both field-term calibrators. \emph{Temperature}
fits $c=\sigma(\ell/T)$ per mask-rate bin on the fit half (a softmax temperature is a
sensitivity row). \emph{Precision} compares 200 LLaDA-8B-Base documents in 4-bit NF4 and fp16.

\subsection{Inference}
\label{sec:inference}

\paragraph{Bootstrap.}
The document is the unit. Intervals are 95\% percentile intervals over \VivaNBoot{} document
resamples, one matrix per run shared by all pool sizes, estimands and arms, so contrast intervals
come from differenced draws. Eq.~2/3 refit per resample, so they use 1,000 (knot panels:
200). Calibrators are not refitted, so E2 intervals condition on the fit half.
% prereg/deviations.md item 7; prereg/analysis_commands.sh (--eq-boot 1000; 200 for knot runs);
% 10,000 resamples of the Eq.~2/3 refits would take weeks per run.

\paragraph{Declaring effects, multiple testing.}
An effect is declared only if its interval excludes 0 \emph{and} its magnitude exceeds twice
the non-determinism floor, the largest change in $\ell$ and $\Delta(m)$ over five repeats of 20
reserve documents per model family. Repeats were bitwise identical, so every floor is
\VivaLladaFloor{} and in practice the interval excludes 0. $\delta_{\min}=\VivaDeltaMin$ is
used for power only.
% PRIMARY_ARMS=(uniform decoder:topk_conf:k4) x 7 ARMS in prereg/analysis_commands.sh;
% scripts/summarize_c1.py (Holm on two-sided bootstrap p-values).
Holm's correction, on two-sided bootstrap $p$-values, runs in three families: the E1 contrast
and the E2 contrast, each over the seven arms of Table~\ref{tab:runs} in the uniform and
$k{=}4$ decoder states (14 tests each), and $\gamma_2,\gamma_3,\gamma_4$ within each arm.
$k\in\{2,8\}$ and matched uniform are exploratory; the contamination-clean and time-split runs
are robustness checks outside the families.

\subsection{Validation}
\label{sec:validation}

\paragraph{Known truth (T0).}
The pipeline was first run on simulated traces in four worlds: calibrated everywhere;
miscalibrated alike in every context; miscalibrated by context (the winner's curse of
Proposition~\ref{prop:split}); and errors shared within a state (violating
Proposition~\ref{prop:select}). The E1 contrast was zero only in the first, and $\beta_z$ without
fixed effects strongly negative only in the third. Three findings shape how we read \S\ref{sec:c1}: the field-term estimator reads high, slightly positive when the truth is zero
and above it otherwise; fixed effects do not fully clean $\beta_z$ in the third world; and
shared errors give a \emph{negative} field term with a \emph{positive} $\beta_z$ without fixed
effects. Too few knots let curvature load onto $z_{r,m}$, so we use \VivaKnots{}.
