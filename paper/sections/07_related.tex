\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Masked diffusion LMs.}
LLaDA and Dream \citep{nie2025llada,ye2025dream} scale masked diffusion \citep{sahoo2024mdlm}
to 7--8B parameters and commit the most confident positions, as MaskGIT did
\citep{chang2022maskgit}. We audit that rule; we propose no model.

\paragraph{Parallel and confidence-based decoders.}
Decoders replace top-1 probability, which can misstate uncertainty \citep{kim2025worst},
with entropy, margin or stability \citep{benhamu2025entropy,kim2025worst,kim2025klass},
tune the cutoff \citep{wu2025fastdllm,shen2025osdt} or learn the policy
\citep{jazbec2026learning}. They evaluate speed and accuracy, not the calibration of the
tokens they commit, which C1 measures; C3a thresholds a recalibrated probability instead
(\S\ref{sec:c3}).

\paragraph{Calibration and the winner's curse.}
Calibration is usually measured over all predictions \citep{guo2017calibration}. Closest in
mechanism, PC-Sampler \citep{huang2025pcsampler}, recalibrates the commit statistic for a
positional bias; we add its weight as a covariate. Keeping the largest of noisy estimates
biases it upward \citep{andrews2024winners}, more so as the pool grows, as in beam search
\citep{dalal2026beam}, but leaves a correct posterior unbiased
\citep{dawid1994selection,efron2011tweedie}, which confines our winner's curse to the field term
(\S\ref{sec:setup}). Diffusion LMs are overconfident against autoregressive models on most
tasks \citep{yadav2026bidirectional}, a Bayesian, entropy-based commit order lowers their
sequence-level calibration error \citep{jiang2026bayeser}, and commit confidence tracks
correctness on math but not on factual recall \citep{asaria2026diffusiongemma}; none of these conditions on how many candidates a committed
token beat.

\paragraph{Dependency between tokens.}
Dependency-aware decoders \citep{kim2026dapd,ringel2026demask,azangulov2026punt} and
ParallelBench \citep{kang2026parallelbench} target term (A) of Eq.~\ref{eq:decomp} and take
the marginals as accurate. The split is not ours: EB-Sampler uses the same two terms
\citep{benhamu2025entropy}, and \citet{tang2026sampler} note that sampler and model error
stay entangled. C2 computes both in nats where $p^*$ is known (\S\ref{sec:c2}).
