\subsection{C2: the main model and T10 details}
\label{sec:c2-app}

% Bar, effects, NELBO and the choice: artifacts/probe/main_variant.json (via macros_t9.tex);
% it records where the bar was first written (commit fc38138, with the results) and that the
% fallback is stated as a rule in no committed file.
\paragraph{Main model.}
One fine-tune masked the $\rho$ marker like any token (\CTwoAltFtTokensM{}M tokens); the main
model never did, as at evaluation (\CTwoFtTokensM{}M). Neither moved the copied value's
probability by our bar of \CTwoMarkerBar{} when the marker is switched from $\rho=1$ to $0$
with only the leader observed (\CTwoAltMarkerEffect{} and \CTwoMarkerEffect), so we took the
lower validation NELBO (\CTwoValNelbo{} against \CTwoAltValNelbo{} nats per token); the bar was
first written down with these results, the fallback only when applied. The other
(Figure~\ref{fig:c2-alt}) crosses over at $k=\CTwoAltConfCrossKatRhoOne$ for every
$\rho\ge\CTwoAltConfCrossRhoMin$ under both rules.

% 256 tokens: config.max_tokens in both T10 JSONs (the C1 length filter); ids: documents.last_doc_id.
\paragraph{T10.}
Unlike the plan, we skip documents under 256 tokens (fit-half ids up to
\CTwoTTenLastDocId; \CTwoTTenLladaLastDocId{} for LLaDA) and keep at least \CTwoTTenMinMasked{}
masked positions per state. Intervals resample documents (\CTwoTTenNBoot{} times).
