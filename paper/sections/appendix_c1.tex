\subsection{C1: E3, controls and committed tokens}
\label{sec:c1-app}

\paragraph{Weighting.}
Averages are taken within a document, every state weighing the same, then across documents;
positions are weighted by $1/(|M_s|\times\text{states in their document})$ to match.

\paragraph{E3, trajectory shift.}
On the positions of decoder states at each $k$ and of matched uniform states (secondary: the
uniform grid, reweighted to the decoder's rate mix), mask rate in $[0.05,0.95)$, we fit
$\Pr(a{=}1)=\sigma(\alpha_b+f(\ell)+\textit{dec}\cdot(g_0+h(\ell)))$, $\alpha_b$ per mask-rate bin (centred on $0.1,\dots,0.9$).
The endpoint $\E_{\mathrm{dec}}[\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(\ell,b)-\pi_{\mathrm{dec}}(\ell,b)]$ is positive if
decoder-state tokens are right less often at equal confidence and mask rate; a sup-$t$ test checks $(g_0,h)$ jointly.

\paragraph{Controls.}
A change of $\Delta$ with $m$ could come from what winners are rather than from winning.
\emph{Position} (absolute position, distance to the nearest unmasked token,
PC-Sampler's position weight; \citealp{huang2025pcsampler}) and \emph{token type} (of the true
token: punctuation, stop-word or content) are covariates $x$: $\E_m[\pi(\ell)-\pi(\ell,x)]$
is removed from $\Delta(m)$ and $x$ enters both field-term calibrators. \emph{Temperature}
fits $c=\sigma(\ell/T)$ per mask-rate bin on the fit half (a softmax temperature is a sensitivity row).
Table~\ref{tab:c1-t8} gives each control. For LLaDA-8B-Base in uniform states the change in E1
% position: 4.4e-5 [2.3e-5, 6.5e-5] in ablation.json; its macro rounds to 0.0000 [0.0000, 0.0001]
is below $0.0001$ for position,
\CresCI{TEightLladaBaseTokenTypeEOneUniDifference} for token type and
\CresCI{TEightLladaBaseTemperatureLogoddsEOneUniDifference} for a temperature per mask-rate bin.
In decoder states E1 keeps its sign under every control in every arm and moves by about
0.03 at most (temperature, LLaDA-8B-Instruct on MBPP: 0.156 to 0.125): the growth is not where
winners sit, what kind of token they are, or an over- or underconfidence by mask rate. In
uniform states the small base and 110M contrasts move by up to 0.004, so their sign is not
robust.

On the report half of the \VivaLladaBaseFourBitDocs{} documents of the 4-bit run (calibrators
from their fit half), 4-bit NF4 in place of fp16 changes LLaDA-8B-Base's E1 by
\CresCI{TEightLladaBasePrecisionPrecisionEOneUniDifference} in uniform states and by
\CresCI{TEightLladaBasePrecisionPrecisionEOneDecTopkConfKFourDifference} in decoder states:
both intervals hold 0, but in decoder states the difference is as large as that cell's E1
in Table~\ref{tab:c1-main}, and fp16 E1 on these documents is itself
\Cres{TEightLladaBasePrecisionPrecisionEOneDecTopkConfKFourWithout}, so a precision effect
there is not ruled out. Decoder-state E2 changes by
\CresCI{TEightLladaBasePrecisionPrecisionETwoDecTopkConfKFourDifference}, of the size of the
base models' E2.

\paragraph{Committed tokens.}
\label{sec:c1-app-native}

For tokens the $k{=}4$ decoder commits with 32--63 positions masked, $c-a$ is
\CresCI{COneLladaInstructGsmNativeThirtyTwoDecTopkConfKFour} for LLaDA-8B-Instruct on GSM8K,
against an ECE of \Cres{COneLladaInstructGsmECEUni} over random masks; it is 0.013 to 0.018 for
the base and 110M models and 0.079 to 0.176 for the instruct models, which reach 0.15 to 0.27
with 128--255 positions masked. Commit-time miscalibration has been reported for whole answers
\citep{jiang2026bayeser}; here it holds per committed token. The mask count mixes field size
with lost context, so this describes and does not test E1.
