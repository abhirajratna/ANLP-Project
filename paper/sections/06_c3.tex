\paragraph{The rule.}
A top-$k$ decoder fixes the number of tokens per step in advance; C3a lets calibrated
confidence set it. At each step it computes $\hat c_i=\hat\pi(\ell_i,s)$ for every masked
position, with the context-aware calibrator of \S\ref{sec:estimands} on three field
features (median $\ell$, mask rate, $\log|M|$), and commits every position with
$\hat c_i\ge 1-\alpha$, at least one. So $\alpha$ is an expected error rate per committed
token, and the one speed knob. Positions are taken in the model's own order of $\ell$, so
C3a changes \emph{how many} tokens are committed, not \emph{which}. It needs no extra
forward pass.

\paragraph{T11: the calibrator.}
One calibrator per task is fitted on teacher-forced decoder states ($k\in\{2,4,8\}$) of the
fit-half LLaDA-8B-Instruct traces (\Cres{CThreeTElevenGsmFitDocs} GSM8K and
\Cres{CThreeTElevenMbppFitDocs} MBPP documents) and frozen. A check inside the fit half (not
a result) puts its ECE against the raw $\sigma(\ell)$ at \Cres{CThreeTElevenGsmDevECECal} from
\Cres{CThreeTElevenGsmDevECERaw} on GSM8K and \Cres{CThreeTElevenMbppDevECECal} from
\Cres{CThreeTElevenMbppDevECERaw} on MBPP. It is then applied to free generation, which starts
from 256 masks; its fit states lie inside a reference answer. \Cres{CThreeTTwelveGsmOutsideShare}
of C3a's GSM8K steps and \Cres{CThreeTTwelveMbppOutsideShare} of its MBPP steps have features
outside the fitted range.

\paragraph{T12: matched speed.}
LLaDA-8B-Instruct generates freely on the report halves of GSM8K
(\Cres{CThreeTTwelveGsmN} problems) and MBPP (\Cres{CThreeTTwelveMbppN}): zero-shot chat prompt,
256 tokens in blocks of 32, greedy, one seed, fp16.
% TODO(check): precision.  run_t12.py defaults to fp16 and jarvis_t12.sh / t12.sbatch force
%   it (the calibrator's traces are fp16); documents/final-plan.md still says bf16 on the
%   A100.  Read "precision" in the runs' meta.json; if bf16, write "in bf16, while the
%   calibrator was fitted on fp16 traces" and say so in the limitations (C3 paragraph).
C3a is compared with confidence top-$k$, Fast-dLLM's threshold \citep{wu2025fastdllm},
margin, entropy-bounded \citep{benhamu2025entropy}, KLASS and PC-Sampler
\citep{huang2025pcsampler}.
% TODO(check): citations for margin (J. Kim et al. 2025, arXiv:2502.06768) and KLASS (S. H. Kim
%   et al. 2025, arXiv:2511.05664) are not in refs.bib; add them.  PC-Sampler runs
%   position-only unless the background corpus was supplied (policies.py PCSampler.variant,
%   jarvis_t12.sh): say which in this sentence.
Each decoder runs at three settings of its own speed knob ($k$, $\tau$, $\gamma$, $\alpha$,
\dots), and speed is measured as mean tokens per step. For each C3a setting, each decoder is
taken at the setting closest in mean tokens per step, within $\pm10\%$, or left out
(Table~\ref{tab:c3-speed}). The accuracy difference is paired on the shared problems, with a
2,000-resample bootstrap interval over problems. This rule was fixed before any report-half
number was read. The sanity check, cost and curves are in Appendix~\ref{sec:c3-app}.
