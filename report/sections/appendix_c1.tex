\subsection{C1: E3, controls and committed tokens}
\label{sec:c1-app}

\paragraph{Weighting.}
% abhiraj, 31 Oct (review): c3/endpoints/e1.py winner_gap (exact m=1, 1/n_in_state); c3/stats.py grouped_doc_mean
%   (documents without rows drop out of point and draws); c3/calibrators.py pool_weights; c3/splines.py default_knots
%   (quantiles 0.05..0.95); c3/logit.py ridge_fe 1e-3; POOL_SIZES includes 1 (c3/__init__.py).
Averages are taken within a document, every state weighing the same, then across documents;
documents without pools drop out. A state's \VivaNPools{} winners at each
$m$ weigh equally, and its positions $1/|M_s|$ at $m=1$. Calibrators weight positions by
$1/(|M_s|\times\text{states of that kind in their document})$; decoder states are fitted per $k$.
Knots sit at evenly spaced quantiles of fit-half $\ell$, 5\% to 95\%. In
Eqs.~\ref{eq:eqtwo}--\ref{eq:eqthree}, $m=1$ rows are the drawn singletons and $\alpha_s$ has a
ridge of $10^{-3}$ on the unweighted log-likelihood.

\paragraph{E3, trajectory shift.}
On the positions of decoder states at each $k$ and of matched uniform states, mask rate in $[0.05,0.95)$, we fit
$\Pr(a{=}1)=\sigma(\alpha_b+f(\ell)+\textit{dec}\cdot(g_0+h(\ell)))$, $\alpha_b$ per mask-rate bin (0.1 wide, centred on $0.1,\dots,0.9$), $f$, $h$ joint splines on \VivaKnots{} shared knots.
The endpoint $\E_{\mathrm{dec}}[\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(\ell,b)-\pi_{\mathrm{dec}}(\ell,b)]$ is positive if
decoder-state tokens are right less often at equal confidence and mask rate; a sup-$t$ test checks $(g_0,h)$ jointly.

\paragraph{Controls.}
\emph{Position} (absolute position, distance to the nearest unmasked token,
PC-Sampler's position weight; \citealp{huang2025pcsampler}) and \emph{token type} (of the true
token: no letter or digit, an NLTK stop-word, or content) are covariates $x$: $\E_m[\hat\pi(\ell)-\hat\pi(\ell,x)]$
is removed from $\Delta(m)$, and E2 becomes $\E_m[\hat\pi(\ell,x)-\hat\pi(\ell,s,x)]$. \emph{Temperature}
fits $c=\sigma(\ell/T)$ per mask-rate bin on the fit half.
Table~\ref{tab:c1-t8} gives each control. For LLaDA-8B-Base in uniform states the change in E1
% position: 4.4e-5 [2.3e-5, 6.5e-5] in ablation.json; its macro rounds to 0.0000 [0.0000, 0.0001]
is below $0.0001$ for position,
\CresCI{TEightLladaBaseTokenTypeEOneUniDifference} for token type and
\CresCI{TEightLladaBaseTemperatureLogoddsEOneUniDifference} for a temperature per mask-rate bin.
In decoder states E1 keeps its sign under every control in every arm and moves by about
0.03 at most (temperature, LLaDA-8B-Instruct on MBPP: 0.156 to 0.125): the growth is not where
winners sit, what kind of token they are, or an over- or underconfidence by mask rate. In
uniform states the small base and 110M contrasts move by up to 0.004, so their sign is not
robust.

On the 4-bit run's report half (\VivaLladaBaseFourBitDocs{} documents, calibrators from its fit
half), NF4 in place of fp16 changes LLaDA-8B-Base's E1 by
\CresCI{TEightLladaBasePrecisionPrecisionEOneUniDifference} (uniform) and
\CresCI{TEightLladaBasePrecisionPrecisionEOneDecTopkConfKFourDifference} (decoder states):
both intervals hold 0, but the latter is as large as fp16 E1 on these documents
(\Cres{TEightLladaBasePrecisionPrecisionEOneDecTopkConfKFourWithout}), so a precision effect
is not ruled out. Decoder-state E2 changes by
\CresCI{TEightLladaBasePrecisionPrecisionETwoDecTopkConfKFourDifference}, of the size of the
base models' E2.
