\section{Selection and confidence (C1)}
\label{sec:c1}

C1 asks whether a committed token's confidence overstates its accuracy more when it won a
larger field, and how much is the field term of Proposition~\ref{prop:split}. Every
number is from the report half.

\paragraph{Arms and claims.}
% Claim rule: prereg/frozen_config.json settled.claim_rule (PR #19, deviations.md 16c,d);
% alpha 0.05, family size 14, a missing member counts as p = 1.
We claim an E1 or E2 effect only if it is declared \emph{and} Holm rejects it at $\alpha=0.05$
in its family: the seven arms in uniform and $k{=}4$ decoder states (\S\ref{sec:inference};
a missing cell counts as $p=1$).
Everything else is exploratory, with intervals and no claims: decoder states at
$k\in\{2,8\}$, matched uniform states, $\beta_z$, $\gamma_k$, E3, the native gap, the controls
and the robustness runs.

\subsection{Does the gap grow with the field?}
\label{sec:c1-main}

Of the
\Cres{COneHolmEOneFamily} E1 tests in Table~\ref{tab:c1-main}, Holm rejects \Cres{COneHolmEOneReject} and
\Cres{COneDeclaredEOne} are declared; \Cres{COneConfirmedEOne} meet both and are claims.
For E2 the counts are \Cres{COneHolmETwoReject}, \Cres{COneDeclaredETwo} and
\Cres{COneConfirmedETwo}.
The signs differ, so we do not pool. E1 is positive and claimed in every $k{=}4$ decoder-state
cell and in uniform states of Dream-7B-Instruct on both tasks: there a token that wins a field of
\VivaPoolMax{} is more overconfident than a random masked position. It is negative and claimed
in uniform states of both base models and of LLaDA-8B-Instruct on GSM8K, where the gap
shrinks with the field. The two cells without a claim, uniform states of LLaDA-8B-Instruct on
MBPP and of the 110M model, are both declared but not claimed
(both $p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=\Cres{COneHolmPEOneLladaInstructMbppUni}$).
% Regen check: COneHolmPEOneMdlmUni must equal COneHolmPEOneLladaInstructMbppUni (Holm step-down
% ties them on v1 and the v1.1 dry run); if they differ, name both.
% Hand-typed from c1_main.tex / c1_shared.tex (regen 9b4edab): 12, 0.005, 0.013,
% ICC 0.006 / 0.036-0.056, Third contrast 0.054-0.100 (c1_main.tex), four of five.  (31 Oct: the E1-size sentence

In decoder states E1 turns positive for both base models and LLaDA-8B-Instruct on GSM8K, and is
claimed there for LLaDA-8B-Instruct on MBPP and the 110M model. Uniform states matched to the
decoder's mask counts (exploratory) keep the base models' negative sign, so the change comes
from which positions stay masked.

\paragraph{How much is the field term?}
% abhiraj, 31 Oct (review): field(1) = COne<Label>FieldOne{Uni,DecTopkConfKFour}: base and Mdlm
%   <= 0.001, uniform <= 0.005, instruct decoder 0.011..0.018.  Replaces the LLaDA-Base worked example.
E2 meets the claim rule in all \Cres{COneHolmETwoFamily} cells and is positive in 12, but only given the fitted calibrators: its intervals omit
their error (\S\ref{sec:inference}). The baseline field$(1)$,
near 0 for calibrators fitted on the averaged states alone (\S\ref{sec:estimands}), is at most
0.005 except in the instruct models' decoder states (0.011 to 0.018), where E2 may also carry an
offset that varies with $\ell$. For the base and 110M
models every $|\mathrm{E2}|$ is below 0.005, of the order of what the estimator read on
simulated traces with no field term (\S\ref{sec:validation}), so we do not read these as a
winner's curse. In seven
of the eight instruct cells E2 is 0.013 to \Cres{COneFieldContrastDreamInstructMbppUni}
(Dream-7B-Instruct, MBPP, uniform states, where E1 is \Cres{COneDreamInstructMbppContrastUni}):
there the field term grows with $m$, as a winner's curse predicts: at equal confidence,
winners of large fields come from contexts where tokens are right less often. The eighth,
LLaDA-8B-Instruct on GSM8K in uniform states, is negative.
% Matched-form E2: the "without" E2 column of tables/c1/c1_t8.tex (c3/controls.py: logistic own and context).
A spline-logistic $\hat\pi(\ell)$, of $\hat\pi(\ell,s)$'s form (Table~\ref{tab:c1-t8}, without a
control), leaves E2's sign in every cell and its size within 0.003 except in uniform states of
Dream-7B-Instruct (GSM8K 0.014, MBPP 0.098), where the forms, not context, carry part of it.
Positions of a state share errors in every arm (within-state ICC of residuals above 0
throughout: at most 0.006 in the base and 110M models, 0.036 to 0.056 in the instruct models),
so the split has its third term (Table~\ref{tab:c1-main}, Third). It is near 0 in the base and 110M
models except LLaDA-8B-Base decoder states, and reaches 0.054 to 0.100 on MBPP and in
uniform states of Dream-7B-Instruct on GSM8K, larger than E2 in four of these five cells. There
winners are right less often than $\hat\pi(\ell,s)$ predicts, so selection acts more within states
(shared errors, or positions that differ at equal $\ell$) than through the field term;
E2 and $\beta_z$ are read with that in mind.

% ------------------------------------------------------------------- beta_z
\subsection{The selection diagnostic $\beta_z$}
\label{sec:c1-beta}

$\beta_z$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:eqtwo}) asks whether winning a larger field predicts correctness beyond
the winner's own log-odds; we read it beside the sign of E2 (\S\ref{sec:validation}).
Without fixed effects it excludes 0 in all 14 cells (Table~\ref{tab:c1-main}) and is negative in
12, and with them its interval holds 0 in 8 of those 12: the pattern of a field effect; E2 is positive in all of them but uniform states of LLaDA-8B-Instruct on GSM8K.
% 31 Oct (space): cut "The two positive cells, LLaDA-8B-Base and 110M decoder states, have E2 near the estimator's bias."
% counts: \ddagger markers of tab:c1-main (regen of 28 Oct; recheck at the final regen)
Holm, run within each arm and kind of state, rejects \Cres{COneHolmGammaReject} of the
\Cres{COneHolmGammaFamily} tests of $\gamma_2,\gamma_3,\gamma_4$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:eqthree}), in
\Cres{COneHolmGammaRows} of the 14 rows, all
positive and all in the instruct models and Dream-7B-Base decoder states: within a state, at
equal confidence, lower-ranked pool members are right more often than the winner, which
Proposition~\ref{prop:select} excludes. Like the ICC, we read this as a
sign of shared errors (or of a calibration slope that varies by state); none is a claim.

% ------------------------------------------------------------------- E3, native gap
\subsection{Decoder states}
\label{sec:c1-e3}

E3 compares accuracy at equal confidence and mask rate in decoder and matched uniform states
(Appendix~\ref{sec:c1-app}); the decoder is teacher-forced, so a difference comes from which
positions remain masked, not from its own mistakes. It is positive, its interval excluding 0,
in every arm and at every $k\in\{2,4,8\}$ (sup-$t$ $p\le0.002$; exploratory): tokens are right less often in the states a decoder visits, by 0.001 to 0.006 in
the base and 110M models and 0.03 to 0.06 in the instruct models, so C3a's calibrator is fitted on
decoder states (\S\ref{sec:c3}).
% ranges and p from tables/c1/c1_e3.tex, matched columns (regen of 28 Oct; recheck)
The decoder's own commits are more overconfident still: with 32--63 positions masked, $c-a$ on the $k{=}4$ decoder's commits is 1.8 to 4.4 times its
arm's random-mask ECE in every arm, its interval above it (descriptive;
Appendix~\ref{sec:c1-app-native}).

\subsection{Controls and robustness}
\label{sec:c1-t8}
\label{sec:c1-robust}

Position, token type and a temperature per mask-rate bin leave decoder-state E1 with its
sign in every arm, but not LLaDA-8B-Base's small uniform-state E1, which token type takes
to 0; a 4-bit check does not rule out an effect of precision (Appendix~\ref{sec:c1-app}). Five checks repeat the contrasts outside the Holm families
(code archive).
\emph{Contamination:} without the \VivaLladaContamFlagged{} LLaDA and \VivaDreamContamFlagged{}
Dream documents the screen flagged (\S\ref{sec:models}), both base models' E1 equal their main
values.
\emph{Time split:} on unseen Wikipedia articles (a quarter of the documents) the small negative
uniform-state E1 of the base models is not reproduced (LLaDA-8B-Base:
\CresCI{COneLladaBaseTimeSplitContrastUni}): seen text, the change of domain, or too small to
detect here; the 110M model's grows (\Cres{COneMdlmTimeSplitContrastUni}); it saw neither set, so
domain, not contamination, moves it. Decoder-state E1 stays positive, its interval excluding 0, for all three.
\emph{Knots:} E2 keeps its sign and declared flag with 5 and 13 knots in \Cres{COneKnotsAgree} of
\Cres{COneKnotsCells} cells.

%   0.000 [-0.004, 0.004]; Mdlm Dec 0.001 (sign flip); other six positive instruct cells 0.013 (LI-GSM dec),
%   0.007 (LI-MBPP dec), 0.005 (DI-GSM uni), 0.013 (DI-GSM dec), 0.098 (DI-MBPP uni), 0.039 (DI-MBPP dec).
\emph{Calibrator form:} with the additive calibrator (\S\ref{sec:estimands}) it does in
\Cres{COneAdditiveAgree} of \Cres{COneAdditiveCells} cells. The two exceptions, claimed cells whose E2
we therefore do not rely on, are uniform states of LLaDA-8B-Instruct on MBPP, where E2 falls from
0.013 to 0.000 (interval holding 0), and 110M decoder states, where it goes from $-0.002$ to $0.001$. The other six
positive instruct-model E2s shrink to 0.005--0.098, two of them (Dream-7B-Instruct on GSM8K,
uniform; LLaDA-8B-Instruct on MBPP, decoder) to 0.005 and 0.007, close to the base models' E2.
