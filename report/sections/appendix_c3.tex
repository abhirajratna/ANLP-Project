\subsection{C3: sanity check, KLASS and speed curves (exploratory)}
\label{sec:c3-app}

Before the comparison, the harness decodes \Cres{CThreeTTwelveSanityN} fit-half GSM8K
problems at Fast-dLLM's settings (5-shot, 256 tokens, blocks of 32; fp16 on an RTX 2080 Ti).
Top-$k$ at $k=1$ scores \CresCI{CThreeTTwelveSanityTopKAcc} against a published 0.793, and the
threshold rule at $\tau=0.9$ scores \CresCI{CThreeTTwelveSanityThresholdAcc} against 0.792
\citep{wu2025fastdllm} (95\% Wilson intervals): both published values lie inside, so the
harness reproduces the two decoders it can be checked on, though our points are higher.
At speed 3 (about 8 tokens per step; Figure~\ref{fig:c3-speed}) C3a trails the threshold rule on
GSM8K (C3a $-$ threshold \CresCI{CThreeTTwelveGsmMatchedDiffThresholdSpeedThree}); costs and the
speed-1 and speed-3 rows are in the code archive.

\paragraph{KLASS.}
Ours differs from the reference \citep{kim2025klass} in three ways, found after scoring: a KL
history per 32-token block (two one-token steps at each block start), the reverse KL direction,
and 32-token blocks, not 64. At its published thresholds it runs at about
2.6 tokens per step, as published; aiming at 4 and 8, the knob path, fixed in advance, lowers
$\tau$ to 0.2--0.29, far below the published range. Its curve in Figure~\ref{fig:c3-speed} describes that path, not
KLASS as intended; Table~\ref{tab:c3-speed} omits it.
